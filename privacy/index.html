Privacy policy

What somaari.in, the Somaari apps and the somaari-video tool collect, store and share. In short: the site sets no cookies and has no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared. YouTube loads only if you press Play on a video.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who runs Somaari

Somaari is a personal project of Ashwin Koppam Raghavendra, in Bengaluru, India. Questions about this policy: hello@somaari.in.

This website (somaari.in)

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking from the site itself. The site has no accounts, sign-ups or forms.
  • Nothing loads from other sites unless you press Play on a video. Fonts, styles, scripts and video thumbnails are served from somaari.in itself.
  • Videos. App pages can play walkthrough videos hosted on YouTube. The player loads only when you press Play, from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com). Once it loads, YouTube and Google receive technical details such as your IP address and browser, and may store data in your browser. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Your theme choice (the Appearance button) is saved in your own browser's local storage. It is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare processes the technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Links to other sites, such as GitHub and YouTube, are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

The Somaari apps

The Somaari apps run on your own computer and don't send your data to Somaari. Apps with optional AI features send only what is needed to the AI provider you choose, and only when you turn that on. Each app's guide explains exactly what it stores on your device and what, if anything, it sends.

somaari-video and YouTube

somaari-video is a personal tool that makes demo videos of the Somaari apps and uploads them to the developer's own Somaari YouTube channel. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it. It uses YouTube API Services.

  • What it accesses: only what it needs to upload videos, set their thumbnails, add them to playlists and confirm it is signed in to the Somaari channel. It asks Google for two permissions (scopes): youtube.upload and youtube.
  • Where it is stored: the sign-in token is kept only on the developer's own computer. There is no server and no database.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • How long: the token is kept until access is revoked or the token is deleted. The tool keeps only the IDs of the videos it uploaded.
  • Revoking access: at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
