Task Planner

somaari-plan

Available AI optional

A planner that runs on your own Raspberry Pi or other Linux machine. Turn Markdown plans, pasted notes or a quick line into dated tasks, with Today, calendar and board views.

Video

Watch on YouTube

Key capabilities

  • Plans in

    Upload a Markdown plan or paste text: headings become steps, list items become tasks, and dates in the text are read by rules, not guessed.

  • Review before saving

    An editable preview of every task, with flagged tasks to check and skipped lines you can restore with one click.

  • Today, Calendar and Dashboard

    Today's list by priority; day, week and month views; and a 7-day board to drag tasks across.

How AI is used

  • Code reads plans first: headings, list items, dates, times and scheduling never need AI.
  • AI only answers quick questions code can't (is this line really a task? what priority? is it a meeting?) and, optionally, rewrites loose prose into task titles.
  • Providers: TypeSafe Jev by default, or an LLM from OpenRouter, Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible server, including one on your own network.
  • With AI on, only the lines being decided go to the provider you chose. Free tiers such as OpenRouter's may log prompts. There's no analytics or telemetry.
  • With AI off (mode = "off"), nothing leaves your Pi. AI settings in the guide

Platforms

  • A Raspberry Pi 5, any Raspberry Pi with a 64-bit OS, or any Linux machine
  • Used from a web browser on your network, on a computer or a phone

What you need

  • Python 3.11 or newer, git and uv
  • Optional: a TypeSafe API key for the default AI mode. Or use an LLM instead: an OpenRouter, Gemini or OpenAI-compatible API key, or an OpenAI-compatible server on your network with no key. Or turn AI off
