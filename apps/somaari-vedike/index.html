Festival Stage Coordinator

somaari-vedike

Coming soon No AI

Run the stage at your apartment or community festival from a phone. Start, pause and skip performances with a countdown, show what is on stage on a lobby TV, post announcements to your residents' Telegram channel and capture photos. It runs on a Raspberry Pi or any computer on your Wi-Fi, with no cloud account.

See plans Ask a question

Free for a single-day event. Pro adds multi-day festivals and more, as a pass for one event or a yearly plan.

Key capabilities

  • Live stage control

    Start, pause, resume, complete and skip performances, with a countdown and an overtime alert, in IST.

  • Residents stay informed

    A high-contrast board for the lobby TV or residents' phones, and announcements with festive cards posted to your Telegram group or channel.

  • Yours, on your network

    Runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 or any computer over Wi-Fi or VPN. The schedule, photos and logs stay on that machine, and organizer controls are PIN-protected.
